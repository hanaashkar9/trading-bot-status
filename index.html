Is the edge still there? Sequential drift monitor

Every completed day adds evidence. A good day pushes the line down, a bad day pushes it up; it never goes below zero and restarts at zero after an alarm. It crosses the amber line (drifting) about once every 6 months even when all is well. It crosses the red line (alarm) about once every 3 years when all is well, and typically 5–9 months after a real loss of edge. Why the forest said yes: the three biggest reasons (SHAP)

For each Kareem+Hanna candidate the Random Forest estimates the chance the trade wins after costs, and admits it above 55%. SHAP splits that estimate into one push per input, measured in probability points from the average candidate (≈50%). Green pushes toward "yes", red toward "no". It explains the model; it never changes a trade.

Market gauges and what to expect

How it works. Every hour the bot reads Binance prices for the 30 most-traded coins. Hanna's multi-speed trend signal buys coins that start trending up; Kareem's CUSUM alarm flags big moves, Hanna's trend picks the side, and a Random Forest keeps only trades with more than a 55% estimated chance of profit. The wallet sizes each bet by volatility, targets 20% yearly volatility, and halves new bets when the market is crowded. Exits: trend flips, stop-loss (about two days' normal move), or 30 days.

Paper wallets fill at the hourly close with the backtest's fees and slippage. The demo wallet sends real market orders to Binance's futures demo exchange; the table above checks its book against the exchange. The AI news score is logged only and never trades.
